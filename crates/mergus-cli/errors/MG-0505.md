# MG-0505 · The rows of a load do not fit its raw tables

- Exit code: 1
- Retryable: no
- Rules: MG-REQ-0511, MG-REQ-0514

## Cause
The rows a load extracted do not match the schema of the table, lack a column of its `key`, or
cannot be cast to the columns of its raw tables. Nothing of the load was written to raw, and the
load is recorded in `ops.loads` as failed with this code. The error names the table and what
Arrow reported.

## Example
the rows of table `sales` cannot be written to raw: Schema error: the rows have no key column `sale_id`

## Fix
Check that the table's `key` names columns the source table has, and that the source's column
types did not change since the last load.
