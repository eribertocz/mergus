# MG-0501 · raw and gold behind the same credentials

- Exit code: 1
- Retryable: no
- Rules: MG-REQ-0501

## Cause
raw and gold must be separate stores, each with its own credentials and retention. With the
`local` or `shared` profile their `location` directories are the same, or one lies inside the
other. With the `cloud` profile they use the same `catalog` secret, or one `location` prefix
holds the other. The error names both zones, the key and the shared value, at the line of the
key of gold.

## Example
`project.toml` line 7: raw and gold are behind the same location `lake`; raw and gold must be
separate stores, each with its own credentials.

## Fix
Give each zone its own store, for example `location = "lake/raw"` in `[raw]` and
`location = "lake/gold"` in `[gold]`, or, with `cloud`, `catalog = "secret:raw_catalog"` and
`catalog = "secret:gold_catalog"` with separate buckets.
