# MG-1309 · A selector is not valid

- Exit code: 1
- Retryable: no
- Rules: MG-REQ-1305

## Cause
A word of the selection of `mergus ls` or `mergus plan`, or of its `--exclude`, is not one of
the selectors of MG-REQ-1305: `<model>`, `<model>+`, `+<model>`, `+<model>+`, `tag:<t>`,
`source:<s>`, `path:<folder>` or `changed`, joined by commas. Nothing is selected.

## Example
`mergus ls tag:` names no tag; `mergus ls owner:ana` uses a method that does not exist.

## Fix
Write the selector as one of the forms above, such as `tag:finance` or `+fact_sales`.
